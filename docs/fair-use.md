# Fair Use & Legal Notice

Open Nami is an open-source tool developed for personal archiving, local offline study, and fair-use research.

---

## Operating Principles

* **Client-Side Automation**: Open Nami operates entirely on your local machine as a command-line client orchestrator for `gallery-dl` and `yt-dlp`. It functions like an automated user-agent or specialized web browser.
* **No Remote Servers or Relay Proxies**: Open Nami does not host, cache, index, or redistribute media files on remote servers. All data transfers occur directly between your device and the platform servers.
* **No DRM Circumvention**: Open Nami does not decrypt proprietary DRM streams or bypass subscription paywalls. It retrieves media accessible through public web protocols or your own authenticated account sessions.

---

## Fair Use Doctrine (17 U.S.C. § 107)

Under Section 107 of the United States Copyright Act and comparable provisions internationally, the fair use of copyrighted material for purposes such as criticism, commentary, news reporting, teaching, scholarship, or research is not an infringement of copyright.

Four factors determine fair use:
1. **The purpose and character of the use**: Open Nami is designed for non-commercial, personal, and research applications.
2. **The nature of the copyrighted work**: Archiving social media posts, public announcements, and educational video content.
3. **The amount and substantiality of the portion taken**: The tool downloads media requested explicitly by the user for offline backup.
4. **The effect upon the potential market**: Personal local backups do not substitute for or harm the original market value of the content.
